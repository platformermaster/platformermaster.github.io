HOW TO PLAY

2D PLATFORMER CONTROLS

  • A / D or Left / Right Arrows: Move Left and Right
  • W / Space / Up Arrow: Jump
  • 1 / 2 / 3: Switch between equipped weapons
  • Mouse Click: Fires the selected weapon

WORKSHOP & MARKET MECHANICS

Equip weapons, torsos, and modules to boost battle power. Fusion items in the Forge to transfer XP and level them up. Battle stages are selected before combat and reward 500 coins for clear.
